# ADR-027 — Provenance levels in reduced form: invariants, ratchet, visibility, and Pinned with per-release approval

**English** · [Русский](../../ru/decisions/ADR-027.md)

**Status:** accepted by the project owner on 2026-10-20; supersedes ADR-005 in part (it adds the Pinned level beside Strict and Attested under the conditions below). Strict and Attested stay as ADR-005 defines them. It accepts proposed ADR-018 in reduced form; the parts of ADR-018 not listed here are not accepted.  
**Date:** 2026-10-20.

## Context

ADR-005 admits a source automatically only in the Strict or Attested mode and rejects pure TOFU for the 0.1 automatic mode. ADR-018 proposed graded levels below Attested: Signed (a publisher signature with a pinned identity) and Pinned (repository-ID pinning and digest freezing only), with level-independent invariants, a ratchet, a time-based quarantine for Pinned, mode limits, and per-level visibility.

R-005 measured what real repositories publish ([R-005 report](../research/R-005-report.md)): only 7% of the latest releases of 100 sampled repositories claimed Strict or Attested evidence, 14% with Signed-claimed rows; the deterministic verified subset of 20 repositories had 3 Strict, 1 Attested, 0 Signed, 1 checksums-only, and 15 with no evidence observed. With ADR-005 alone most of the sample would stay `observing`. R-005 did not test a quarantine or the handling of changed assets, and its theoretical 100% Pinned eligibility is not evidence that Pinned is safe. The Signed forms have not been verified: R-003 is complete only for its read-only part and does not yet cover cosign or OpenPGP checksum signatures.

The project owner accepted a reduced variant of ADR-018 on 2026-10-20.

## Decision

1. **Strict and Attested** stay unchanged (ADR-005, `architecture/trust.md`). They are claimed only after R-003 is complete and the production verifier is selected (ADR-017 point 9, ADR-026).
2. **Invariants** (ADR-018 point 4). Independent of the level and never relaxed: repository-ID pinning; immutability of accepted bytes; family and system-package protection (ADR-022); CAS verification before serving; signed local indexes; the system RPM signature policy (ADR-006, ADR-023, R-002); no re-signing of third-party packages; no disabling of global signature checks; no automatic `yes` for a risky transition.
3. **Ratchet** (ADR-018 point 5). The level of a source is a floor. It may rise automatically when a release carries stronger verified evidence. A release with weaker evidence than the floor (for example, no attestation after attested releases) is held with `E_EVIDENCE_DOWNGRADE` until the user decides. The level never drops silently.
4. **Pinned in 0.1.** The Pinned level has no cryptographic provenance: only repository-ID pinning and digest freezing protect it. It is allowed in 0.1 only when both conditions hold:
   1. **Per-source consent.** The administrator enables Pinned for that source explicitly, after Pakomichi names the risk: a compromised publisher account, token, or CI can publish a malicious release that Pakomichi cannot detect.
   2. **Approval of every release.** Every release of a Pinned source is published only after explicit manual approval by the administrator. There is no automatic publication of Pinned releases. Until approval the release is held with `E_RELEASE_APPROVAL_REQUIRED`. Approval binds to the exact digests of the approved artifacts; a changed asset after approval is `E_ASSET_MUTATED` (digest freezing).

   Consent and approval are administrative actions: they are never available through the read-only status API or the loopback HTTP endpoint (ADR-009), and never through `--yes` or another automatic confirmation.
5. **Mode limits** (ADR-018 point 7). A Pinned source works only in `supplemental` mode, never in `preferred`, and never displaces a distribution package: a name that belongs to the system namespace (ADR-022) or is installed from a distribution source is never activated from a Pinned source, not even by an explicit migration. Multi-machine distribution is outside 0.1 and is not decided here.
6. **Visibility** (ADR-018 point 8). Each level is served from its own logical repository (ADR-007) with a distinct APT `Label`/`Origin` and a distinct DNF repository ID and `name`, so that `apt policy` and `dnf repolist` show the level without `pchi`. The status API and the CLI show the level, the floor, and the reason for every hold.
7. **Deferred to 0.2.** The time-based quarantine for Pinned (ADR-018 point 6) is deferred to 0.2; in 0.1 the approval of every release replaces it. The Signed level (ADR-018 point 2) is deferred to 0.2 pending the extended R-003 (cosign and OpenPGP checksum signatures). Each needs a new owner decision.
8. **Schema.** The Pinned level has no cryptographic evidence, so it is not a `trust_evidence` type. It is recorded in migration 0001, which has not shipped (as for ADR-020): the source's trust floor (`pinned` < `attested` < `strict`, which a trigger never lets drop) and the time of Pinned consent on `forge_repository`, and an append-only `release_approval` table bound to the source, the release, and the exact SHA-256. An artifact carries either evidence or an approval, never both, and an approval can be attached only while the source has Pinned consent.

## Alternatives and reasons for rejection

- **Keep ADR-005 unchanged:** the strongest guarantees, but by R-005 most target repositories would stay `observing`.
- **ADR-018 in full for 0.1 (Signed and Pinned with a quarantine):** Signed is not verified by R-003, and neither the quarantine nor mutation handling was tested by R-005.
- **Pinned with automatic publication after a quarantine only:** a stolen token still reaches every machine without a human looking at the release; deferred to 0.2.
- **TOFU with automatic updates and no quarantine or approval:** turns a stolen token into silent automatic distribution. Rejected, as in ADR-018.

## Consequences

- A consented Pinned source can still deliver a malicious release if the administrator approves it. Consent, approval of every release, the ratchet, and visible labels reduce this risk; they do not remove it, and the documentation and release notes say so.
- Every Pinned release needs administrator work; this is the accepted cost of Pinned in 0.1.
- ADR-018 stays proposed for the parts not accepted here. ADR-005 is not rewritten; its status line points here.
- Updated in both languages: `architecture/trust.md`, `architecture/policy-engine.md`, `architecture/states.md`, `architecture/data-model.md`, `specifications/cli-api.md`, `product/scope-0.1.md`, `testing/negative.md`, `testing/release-gate.md`, and core rule 5 of both READMEs. The reason codes `E_EVIDENCE_DOWNGRADE` and `E_RELEASE_APPROVAL_REQUIRED` are defined in `specifications/errors.md` and `ReasonCode` by the reason-code task.

## Verification

- Evidence: [R-005 report](../research/R-005-report.md), conclusions 1–5; [ADR-018](ADR-018.md).
- Required negative tests in the implementation: a Pinned source is refused `preferred` and never displaces a distribution package (N-037); a release with weaker evidence than the floor is held with `E_EVIDENCE_DOWNGRADE` (N-038); a Pinned release without approval is not published, and an asset changed after approval is `E_ASSET_MUTATED` (N-039); consent or approval through the status API, HTTP, or `--yes` is refused (N-040). None has run yet, because the code does not exist.
