# ADR-037 — Evidence reuse between synchronizations and holding only new family members (refines ADR-022, ADR-030, ADR-034)

**English** · [Русский](../../ru/decisions/ADR-037.md)

**Status:** accepted by the project owner on 2026-10-25 ("I agree with the recommendations"). Point 1 refines when ADR-030 and ADR-034 evidence is looked up again. Point 2 supersedes, for families pinned before, the rule "a family is pinned whole or not at all" of ADR-022 point 1 and of `specifications/apt.md`. It supersedes nothing else.  
**Date:** 2026-10-25.

## Context

- Every `pchi sync` asks GitHub again for the evidence of every listed release: the attestations of each asset, the tag reference, and the peeled commit, about three requests an asset. Without a token GitHub allows 60 requests an hour per external address, and a conditional request answered `304 Not Modified` still counts (measured on 2026-10-25: 34 → 33). With four or five releases the P3-007a test bed ran out of that budget on the third synchronization in an hour (`E_FORGE_UNAVAILABLE`). A receiver with a synchronization timer would too.
- The pin gate of 2026-10-25 holds a family whole when any member cannot be pinned. When a new member of a family that is already pinned has an equal version in another source, the pins of the installed members are removed too, and until the user decides, another source's higher version can take them over under the ordinary update command (ADR-022 point 3).

## Decision

1. **Verified evidence of the same bytes is reused; a full check runs at least daily.**
   - An artifact whose asset ID and SHA-256 were recorded before in the same source with Strict or Attested evidence keeps that evidence without asking GitHub. Attested evidence is reused only when its signer is the source's current Attested signer and its ref is the one the signer's ref rule gives for this release (ADR-036 point 1).
   - A full check asks GitHub for every listed release as before. It runs on the first synchronization of a source, when the last full check of the source is 24 hours old or older, and on `pchi sync --force`. The time of the last full check is stored per source.
   - A result without evidence (`Nothing`) or an invalid one is never reused: it is asked again on every synchronization.
   - Every synchronization still reconciles the repository identity by its ID and reads the release listing, so a transfer, a replacement, a deleted release, and a replaced asset (new bytes behind the same asset ID, `E_ASSET_MUTATED`) are seen at once.
2. **A family pinned before keeps the pins of its pinned members; only new members wait.**
   - The members of a family are split into the pinned ones (an open activation) and the new ones. The new members are pinned together or not at all: when one of them is not listed by APT yet or has an equal version in another source, all new members are held, with the reason, and the pinned members keep their pins.
   - When one of the pinned members has an equal version in another source, the whole family is held, as ADR-022 point 1 requires: that pin itself could raise a file of the other source.
   - A family with no pinned member is handled as before: pinned whole or not at all.

## Alternatives and reasons for rejection

- **A token required for frequent synchronization:** shifts the cost to every receiver and still re-asks the same questions. Right as an option for many sources; not as the only way to stay under the limit.
- **Conditional requests (ETag):** an unauthenticated `304` still counts against the limit.
- **Reusing evidence without a scheduled full check:** a deleted attestation or a moved tag would never be noticed.
- **Keeping the whole-family rule for families pinned before:** removes the protection of installed members because of a member that is not installed yet.

## Consequences

- A deleted attestation or a release tag moved after verification is noticed at the next full check, within 24 hours or at `pchi sync --force`. The bytes are the same in the meantime, and the reused bundle was verified offline against the trusted roots when it was recorded.
- Migration 0006 stores the time of the last full evidence check per source. `pchi sync --force` forces a full check.
- A synchronization without a full check costs two requests plus about three for each asset not seen before.
- Limit of point 2: when a pinned member's new version depends on a held new member, APT resolves that dependency from wherever it finds the member, which can be the other source's equal version. The held member is shown with `E_SOURCE_AMBIGUOUS` in the operation outcome and in the status, so the user sees it before upgrading.
- `specifications/apt.md`, `specifications/cli-api.md`, and `architecture/trust.md` describe the rules in both languages.

## Verification

- Tests: a second synchronization of the same bytes asks GitHub nothing for their evidence; a full check runs when the last one is 24 hours old and on `--force`; a changed signer or another ref is not reused; a result without evidence is asked again; a replaced asset is still `E_ASSET_MUTATED`; a new member with an equal version elsewhere is held alone while the pinned members keep their pins; an equal version of a pinned member holds the whole family.
- The P3-007a test bed runs its passes within one hour without waiting for the budget.
